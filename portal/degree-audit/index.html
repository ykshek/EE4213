課程結構與學期規劃Curriculum and term plan

課程會按建議學期分組。展開學期後，可將課程列拖至另一學期摘要以移動課程。課程列右側的圖示按鈕可直接標記「已完成」或「不合格」，亦可重設學期；需要選擇科目或查看先修資訊時，使用「⋯」開啟選項浮窗。先修科目不合格時，受阻課程會顯示先修鏈。 Courses are grouped by recommended semester. Expand a semester, then drag a course row onto another semester summary to move it. Use the icon buttons on the right of a row to mark it Completed or Failed or to reset its semester. Use the ⋯ button to open an options popover for slot selection or prerequisite details. When a prerequisite fails, blocked courses show the prerequisite chain.
