學習範疇Study areas

▦01 / HARDWARE

電子及微電子Electronics & Microelectronics

研究電子電路、電子器件及集成電路，應用於電子產品、晶片及硬件系統。Explore electronic circuits, devices, and integrated circuits used in products, chips, and hardware systems.

⌁02 / CONNECTIVITY

通訊及無線技術Communications & Wireless

了解訊號傳輸、天線及新一代流動通訊技術。Learn about signal transmission, antennas, and next-generation mobile communication technologies.

ϟ03 / ENERGY

電力及能源系統Power & Energy Systems

認識電力供應、能源轉換及智能電網等技術。Study electricity supply, energy conversion, and smart-grid technologies.

✳04 / INTELLIGENCE

人工智能及訊號處理AI & Signal Processing

接觸數據分析、機器學習、訊號處理及智能系統。Explore data analytics, machine learning, signal processing, and intelligent systems.

◎05 / CONNECTED WORLD

物聯網及跨領域應用IoT & Interdisciplinary Applications

了解連網裝置如何收集及交換資料，應用於智能家居、工業監測及智慧城市。See how connected devices collect and exchange data in smart homes, industrial monitoring, and smart cities.

四年學習旅程YOUR FOUR-YEAR JOURNEY

由基礎知識，走向實際工程項目。From fundamentals to real engineering projects.

四年學習概覽僅供參考。An illustrative overview of four years of study.

YEAR 01
01

建立基礎Build the foundations

學習數學、科學及工程基礎，初步認識電機工程和基本編程。Develop foundations in mathematics, science, and engineering, with an introduction to electrical engineering and basic programming.

YEAR 02
02

掌握核心知識Learn core engineering

進一步學習電子學、電路、訊號及系統，並透過實驗連結理論與應用。Study electronics, circuits, signals, and systems, using practical work to connect theory with applications.

YEAR 03
03

探索專業方向Explore a specialisation

按主修及個人興趣深入學習，接觸工程設計、分析及應用。Develop deeper knowledge in your selected major and areas of interest through engineering design, analysis, and applications.

YEAR 04
04

整合知識及完成專題Bring it together

完成畢業專題（Final Year Project），運用所學分析及解決工程問題。Complete a Final Year Project and apply what you have learned to an engineering problem.

理論與實踐並重LEARN BY DOING

把課堂知識，變成解決問題的能力。Turn classroom knowledge into problem-solving skills.

除了課堂理論，學生亦會透過實驗、編程與模擬、小組設計及畢業專題，了解工程技術如何應用於實際問題。Alongside theory, students learn through laboratory work, programming and simulation, team design activities, and a Final Year Project—connecting engineering knowledge with practical problems.

01

實驗室學習Laboratory learning使用儀器、電路及軟件工具觀察和測試工程系統。Use instruments, circuits, and software tools to observe and test engineering systems.

02

編程及模擬Programming & simulation利用程式及模擬工具分析系統表現。Use programming and simulation tools to analyse system performance.

03

小組及設計項目Team and design projects練習合作、技術溝通及解決問題。Practise teamwork, technical communication, and problem-solving.

04

畢業專題Final Year Project在導師指導下完成工程相關研究或設計項目。Complete an engineering-related research or design project under academic supervision.

職涯方向Career paths

以下為職業例子，職責因僱主和職位而異。Example roles. Responsibilities vary by employer and position.

▣

電子／硬件工程師Electronics / Hardware Engineer

設計、測試及改良電子電路或硬件產品。Design, test, and improve electronic circuits or hardware products.

⌁

電訊工程師Telecommunications Engineer

參與通訊網絡、無線系統及訊號傳輸工作。Work on communication networks, wireless systems, and signal transmission.

ϟ

電力及能源系統工程師Power & Energy Systems Engineer

參與電力系統、能源管理及電網項目。Contribute to power systems, energy management, and grid projects.

◈

半導體／IC 設計工程師Semiconductor / IC Design Engineer

參與晶片、集成電路及半導體器件的設計或測試。Work on chip design, integrated circuits, or semiconductor-device testing.

✳

人工智能及訊號處理工程師AI & Signal Processing Engineer

運用數據、演算法及訊號處理技術開發智能系統。Apply data, algorithms, and signal-processing techniques to intelligent systems.

⌘

工程研究及開發人員Engineering R&D

參與新技術研究、產品開發及系統測試。Contribute to technology research, product development, and system testing.

適合對科技充滿好奇的你COULD THIS BE YOUR PATH?

你喜歡探索事物如何運作嗎？Curious about how things work?

如果你對數學、物理、科技或編程有興趣，喜歡分析問題、動手實驗，並願意持續學習新技術，電機工程可能值得你進一步了解。入學前不需要精通所有工程技術。If you enjoy mathematics, physics, technology, or programming—and like analysing problems, experimenting, and learning new things—Electrical Engineering may be worth exploring. You do not need to master engineering technologies before entering university.
