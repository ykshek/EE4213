2025/26 主修分配摘要 2025/26 Major Allocation Summary

以下為 2025/26 學年四年制入學學生在一年級結束後的分配安排。 This summary covers the 2025/26 allocation exercise for four-year degree students at the end of their first year.

第一階段：前 40%Phase one · Top 40%

按一年級 B 學期結束時的累積平均績點（CGPA）排名，符合校方條件的前 40% 學生獲派第一志願。條件包括在 A、B 學期沒有不及格科目，並完成至少 30 學分（包括指定課程）。 Ranking uses the cumulative GPA (CGPA) at the end of Year 1 Semester B. Students in the top 40% who meet the university’s conditions receive their first choice. The conditions include passing all courses and completing at least 30 credit units across Semesters A and B, including specified courses.

第二階段：其餘 60%Phase two · Remaining 60%

分配考慮一年級 B 學期結束時的累積平均績點、志願次序、各主修學額及其甄選準則。 Allocation considers the CGPA at the end of Year 1 Semester B, students’ ranked preferences, available places and each major’s selection criteria.
